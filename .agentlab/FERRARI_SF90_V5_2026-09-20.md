## Why Ferrari V4 can still sound synthetic

V4 improved V3 layering but retained three causes of a synthetic/"musical" engine quality:

1. Body still includes an independent carrier at 48 + 0.018*rpm. It is useful as a
   low-frequency support oscillator, but it behaves more like a swept tone than a
   pressure-excited structure.
2. The extra V4 cavity/intake material uses explicit cycle(fire*1.37/1.43/2.61/2.73).
   Even with frequency windows, these are additional RPM-tracking pitches.
3. The four outer V3 groups are broad LP paths. Therefore the source must already
   be structurally separated; adding more tonal partials inside Character does not
   create a physical-looking cavity response.

Ferrari V2 sounded more pressure-like because a raised-cosine fifth-power pulse
excited Body/Cavity/Mechanical paths, including pressure-correlated broadband
material. Random combustion texture now correctly belongs to independent Noise,
so V5 restores the pressure/structure relationship without putting random noise
back into DNA.

### Body / Cavity / Mechanical

A cycle-normalized deterministic pressure edge plus the pressure pulse excites
fixed band-pass modes:

- Body: 123 Hz Q2.3, 185 Hz Q3.2, 214 Hz Q2.8.
- Cavity: 240 Hz Q2.6, 620 Hz Q2.1.
- Mechanical: 820 Hz Q1.8, 1100 Hz Q1.6.

These are artistic/analysis-derived structure modes, not a measured physical IR.
They do not move proportionally with RPM. At idle the pressure harmonics naturally
place 2E/3E around 123/185 Hz; at higher RPM the pulse edges continue to excite the
structure instead of dragging the whole structure upward.

Load envelopes:
- Core attack/release 30/120 ms.
- Body 60/260 ms.
- Cavity 45/200 ms.
- Mechanical 18/75 ms.
- Full lift applies about 4–5 dB fast reduction to the upper mechanical return,
  while Body/Cavity disappear according to their slower load envelopes.

## Local listening sequence

1. Ferrari DNA Solo, Noise/SC/Rumble off. Compare SF90 Material 0/1 at:
   740, 1600, 2600, 4200, 6500, 8000 rpm; effort .1/.5/1.
2. At 740 rpm listen for pressure cadence and 120–250 Hz mass without a new sine drone.
3. At 4200 rpm verify the ~350 Hz firing identity remains readable while 120–600 Hz
   structure still carries weight.
4. Accelerate, quick lift, slow lift, repeated shifts. Upper texture should fall first;
   Body/Cavity should not vanish instantly.
5. Then enable Noise at the user's existing level. Noise must add material, not rescue
   a hollow DNA.
6. Only after DNA passes, restore Ferrari Soundscape and final Mix/EQ.

Level-match A/B offline within about ±0.5 dB for listening; retain unnormalized
renders for dynamics and band-energy measurements.
